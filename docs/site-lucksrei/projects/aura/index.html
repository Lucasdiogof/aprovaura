Aprovaura — Study app for ENEM and entrance exams

Questions, mock exams, mistake review and essays in a single study flow.

Draft of November 24, 2026, based on the verified facts of Stage 09. It describes how the app works and is not legal advice.

What it is

Aprovaura is a study app for ENEM, university entrance exams and tests: questions by subject, custom mock exams, mistake review and essay practice in a single flow. One account across Android, iOS and web; initial market: Brazil.

Features

Question bankQuestions organized by subject in a tree of topics and subtopics, with a difficulty filter and a progress bar.
Current eventsDossiers with context, consequences and sources, each with its own practice.
Custom mock examsSubjects, difficulty and number of questions of your choice, with exam mode.
Mistake reviewWrong answers grouped by subject and topic.
AI essay gradingEstimated score by competency (Gemini), with a privacy notice. Not the official score.
Interactive mapsLocate states, countries, rivers and cities, plus flag identification.
Aura and AurudoEach correct answer earns Aura, which sets your level; the mascot Aurudo reacts to goals and streaks.
Daily goal and streakA goal for the day and a streak of study days.
Three languages, two themesPortuguese, English and Spanish; light and dark theme.

3 free days, no card. The trial lasts 72 hours after your e-mail is confirmed, asks for no card and creates no automatic charge. People who already have an account do not get a new trial. Aprovaura does not promise approval in any exam: it is a study aid. Details in Subscriptions.

FAQ

Do I need a card to try it?

No. The 72-hour trial needs no card and has no automatic charge.

Is essay grading the official score?

No. It is an estimate generated by artificial intelligence, may contain mistakes and does not replace the official evaluation of any exam.

Can I use the same account on several devices?

Yes, the account is shared across Android, iOS and web.

How do I cancel?

Through the platform where you subscribed (Google Play, App Store or web). Step by step in Subscriptions.

I am a teacher. Can I be a partner?

Partnership is by invitation. See For partner teachers.

How do I delete my account?

Inside the app, at Profile → My account. See Account and data deletion.
