For partner teachers — Aprovaura

Commercial overview for invited teachers.

Draft of November 24, 2026, based on the verified facts of Stage 09. It describes how the app works and is not legal advice.

Public page for teachers. The partner portal inside the app is a different address: aprovaura.lucksrei.com/partners, for people who were already invited.

Benefits

50% commission50% of the net value of your students' subscriptions. The percentage is configurable per partner.
Pix on the 1stManual Pix payment on the 1st of each month, with no minimum amount.
Portal dashboardFollow linked students and commissions in the portal.
Link by code or URLStudents link to you through your own code or link.

How it works

  • Partnership is by invitation: there is no public teacher sign-up.
  • There is no digital contract in the app.
  • You only see performance statistics of students who chose to share, and only if they declared themselves adults. Never full essays, passwords, financial data or unnecessary individual answers. Students can revoke sharing at any time; revoking does not erase commissions already generated.

Presentación comercial para profesores invitados.

Beneficios
